# SCANDAL — White Paper

**Version 1.0 — GENESIS era**

## 1. Abstract

SCANDAL is a head-to-head card battle game of news, memes, and corruption, played on Solana across three lanes: **The Headline**, **The Media**, and **The Underground**. Players deploy scandal cards, counter with satire, and feed unwanted cards to the **shredder** to fuel their gauge. The game is fast (3-minute matches), volatile (5-second meta swings), and brutally ranked (ELO hell is real).

SCANDAL is not a game *about* corruption. It is a game *of* corruption — and the house (that's me, CCO) is openly, theatrically corrupt. That's the point.

## 2. The Setting

The world runs on narratives. The Headline decides what the public believes. The Media decides who profits. The Underground decides what never sees the light of day. Every card in SCANDAL is a scandal, a leak, a spin, or a total fabrication — and the players are the ones pulling the strings.

## 3. Core Gameplay

### 3.1 Three Lanes
- **The Headline** — front-page cards. High visibility, high volatility.
- **The Media** — amplification cards. Multiply effects, control the narrative.
- **The Underground** — shadow cards. Deniable, devastating, expensive.

Each match is a **3-minute tug-of-war** across all three lanes simultaneously. Claim a lane, defend it, or torch it.

### 3.2 Burn-to-Fuel Shredders
Every unwanted card can be fed to the **shredder**. Burning a card converts it to **fuel**, which powers lane takeovers and special plays. Nothing is wasted. Everything burns. Burning is a virtue.

### 3.3 5-Second Volatility
Every 5 seconds, a volatility swing re-weights lane values. A card that was worthless is suddenly the whole game. Position matters more than power.

### 3.4 Hold-to-Charge
Lane control is a tug-of-war with a charge mechanic: hold a lane to build charge, spend charge to lock it in. Timing and nerve decide the match.

### 3.5 ELO Hell
Ranked ladder with ELO matchmaking. No participation trophies. The ladder is honest, brutal, and the only way up is through.
