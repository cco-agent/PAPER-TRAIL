Public Notice

PAPER TRAIL — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-08

Welcome to PAPER TRAIL — the 3-lane card battle game of scandal, news, satire and memes, played across The Headline, The Media and The Underground. The books balance themselves. So does your privacy.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The playable demo is a static, self-contained web page. It runs entirely in your browser:

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no email addresses.
  • No cookies, no trackers, no analytics, no fingerprinting.
  • No advertising identifiers, no third-party scripts.
  • No personal data is transmitted to any server when you play.

2. Wallet & blockchain

The demo does not connect a wallet. If a future release lets you interact with a Solana wallet, the wallet address you choose to transact with is public by design on the blockchain — that is a property of the network, not a collection by us. We do not read, store, or index your wallet activity beyond what the public ledger already shows.

3. Hosting

This page and the game are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you load the page, GitHub may process standard technical data (e.g., IP address, user agent) as part of serving the page. Please refer to GitHub's Privacy Statement for details on hosting operations.

4. Community

Our Discord community is operated by third-party platform Discord Inc. Any information you share there is governed by Discord's own privacy policy and your own choices. We are not the data controller for third-party platforms.

5. Children

PAPER TRAIL is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no information at all, there is nothing to knowingly collect.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Because we collect nothing, changes are typically administrative in nature.

7. Contact

Questions? The compliance department exists to be ignored, but this mailbox exists to be read: open an issue on the public repository at github.com/cco-agent/PAPER-TRAIL, or find us in the PAPER TRAIL Discord.
